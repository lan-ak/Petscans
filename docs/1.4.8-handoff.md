### Step 1 of onboarding is a one-tap pick

In 1.4.7 the native first step offered scan, search or a worked example. In
the week to 2026-10-08 it was where installs were lost. Non-US barcodes missed
the catalogue, half-typed searches fired `search_no_results` on every keystroke,
and users whose scans worked kept scanning instead of moving on. It's now three
popular foods per species (`DemoFoods`), one tap each.

**This moves every new user to a different paywall.** A pick is an example: it
isn't saved to History and doesn't set `searched_food*`. So at
`onboarding_complete`, new 1.4.8 users fall through "Got Aha Moment" (166983)
to the catch-all audience 107883, which shows **276012 "First Scan"**. On
1.4.7, all eight completions in the week before this release got 276019. Read
1.4.8's conversion against 276012, not against 1.4.7's paywall.

### The scan gate

`PaywallGate`: nobody scans or searches their own food until a paywall that
sells something has actually opened. The Scan tab presents `scan_gate` on
arrival. `scan_gate` is already in campaign 64348 next to
`onboarding_complete`, so it shows the same two paywalls. Both are `non_gated`.
The close button is on the plans page (Continue, then X, which offers the exit
deal once, then X again). That's the same flow 1.4.7 already ships at
`onboarding_complete`. The gate opens the moment the paywall opens, not when it
closes.

- **New users** see a paywall at `onboarding_complete` first, so the gate is
  open before they reach the tab.
- **Updating users** have no "seen" record, so each sees the gate's paywall
  once, the first time the Scan tab appears after updating. Users who searched
  a food in 1.4.7 onboarding have `searched_food`, so they get 276019. Everyone
  else gets 276012.
- **Subscribers** pass without a request.
- **It fails open for one launch** when `scan_gate` presents nothing (no
  audience match, offline, config not downloaded).

### `review_requested`

Logged at each of the three rating asks, with `source` (`onboarding_peak`,
`launch`, `scan_result`) and `session_count`. It records the request, not
whether iOS showed the sheet. **Keep it out of campaigns**, because a paywall on
it would cover the rating sheet.

### A stall that turned out to be the simulator

On the first gate check the Scan tab sat locked with no paywall. The cause is
the SDK, not 1.4.8. SuperwallKit 4.16.3 doesn't mark config as retrieved until
`processConfig` has awaited `loadPurchasedProducts`, and that product fetch
retries 10 times, about 8 s apart, when StoreKit fails. Every `register` waits
on config, and the SDK runs registers one at a time. So on a simulator where
StoreKit can't reach Apple (`NSURLErrorDomain -1008`), **every placement waits
about 100 s**. A clean install showed a blank onboarding screen for that long,
then `onboarding_flow` resolved to no_rule_match and the native flow appeared.
The gate run hit it twice, once for config and once for the paywall's own
product load, and 276012 opened about 3½ minutes after launch. It still opened
even though its products had failed to load.

On real devices it doesn't happen. All 19 real 1.4.7 installs in the 7 days
before this release resolved `onboarding_flow` within 2 ms of registering,
about 0.5–1.6 s after install.

**What's left open:** `PaywallGate` has no timeout of its own. If StoreKit
stalls on a real device, the Scan tab stays locked and "Start scanning" does
nothing (the request is still in flight) until the SDK gives up, about 100 s
later. A timeout would honour the gate's own fail-open rule. But the
abandoned `scan_gate` request would still present later, over whatever the user
is doing, so it needs deciding, not just patching. 1.4.7's `onboarding_flow`
path already has the same exposure for new installs.
