## About the product

PetScans is a free iPhone app. You point your camera at any pet food or treat (barcode or
ingredients label) and get an instant **0–100 safety score** in about 3 seconds. You add your pet's
profile once — their name, breed, and any ingredients they're allergic to — and the app then **flags
those ingredients on every product you scan**. Download: search "PetScans" on the App Store.

## The idea

Most pet parents have no idea their pet's food contains something the pet reacts to (chicken is the #1
food allergy in dogs and cats). This video is your real, relatable story: your pet had a problem →
you discovered it was an ingredient → now you scan food before you buy it.

## Who we're looking for

- **Ideal:** your pet has (or had) a food allergy, itchy skin, ear issues, or a sensitive stomach.
- If not, you can still tell it as "I never knew what was in his food until…" — authentic curiosity works too.

## Tone & style
Warm, casual, honest. Natural lighting, home setting, your pet in frame. Like a story you'd text a friend
— NOT an ad read. Slightly imperfect is better than polished.

## Do
- Show your real pet and your real reaction.
- Say the app name "PetScans" clearly at least once.
- Keep energy up in the first 3 seconds — that's what stops the scroll.
- Add captions (most people watch on mute).

## Don't (important — these get videos rejected)
- ❌ No health/medical claims — don't say it "cures," "treats," "heals," "prevents disease," or "guarantees" anything.
- ❌ Don't call it vet advice or say it diagnoses your pet.
- ❌ No before/after health transformations or weight-loss framing.
- ✅ Keep it about **ingredients and allergen alerts**, not curing conditions. ("It flags what he's allergic to" = good. "It cured his allergies" = not allowed.)
