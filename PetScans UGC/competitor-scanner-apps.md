# Competitor Scanner Apps — Full Detection Reference (fully linked)

Companion to `pet-food-scanner-tiktok-landscape.md`. Every ingredient/food-scanner app detected across the 8 research queries (363 videos), with its best-performing video **linked** and its engagement read. App detection comes from Gemini identifying an in-video `app_promotion`.

**Read the tiers:** `organic_hit` = genuine engagement (>5% of viewers) · `weak` = 0.3–3% · `likely_paid`/`low` = big views, ~0% engagement = boosted spend, not organic love. **For a scanner app, `likely_paid` is a red flag, not a win.** Follower counts are shown so you can separate a real creator's mention from a brand account talking to itself.

**The one-line conclusion:** the dedicated pet-scanner category is crowded with tiny brand-owned promo accounts that don't travel; the *human* scanners (Yuka / Bobby Approved / Oasis) own the reach and have trained the behavior — but none of them personalize to the individual pet.

> ⚠️ **Data caveat:** `outpost`'s automatic App-Store matching returned 0 confirmed store links this run (Apple's iTunes lookup API was rate-limiting us — HTTP 429). So every app below is identified from **in-video detection**, not App Store metadata. Cross-check each in the App Store before treating it as a formal competitor.

## C. Food brands that dominate the conversation (villains & heroes)

Useful for choosing which products to *scan on camera* — scanning a brand people already argue about is free reach.

**Lightning-rod villains (attacked → viral):**
- **Purina Pro Plan / Purina One** — by far the most-named, and *both attacked and defended* (a built-in controversy engine): [attack, 2.13M](https://www.tiktok.com/@shannnerss/video/7634336513945357598) · [Purina One callout, 2.86M](https://www.tiktok.com/@t__reavor/video/7646731272433224991) · [ingredient breakdown, 1.94M](https://www.tiktok.com/@caninerevolution_/video/7636464865825049870) · [defense "shiny coat of a dog fed 'poison'", 1.31M](https://www.tiktok.com/@ebbnflowbcs/video/7631707402374548749).
- **Pedigree** — [recall, 581K](https://www.tiktok.com/@niickjackson/video/7661412161780075789).
- **Ol' Roy / Special Kitty (Walmart budget brands), Whiskas, Kirkland** — recurring "worst/deadliest" targets.

**Heroes / "upgrade to this" (often paid partnerships — note the `weak`/`solid` tiers = boosted):**
- **KatKin** ([@two.islam, 6.87M, paid, `weak`](https://www.tiktok.com/@two.islam/video/7653154363019611395)), **ACANA** ([@alondrarubyyy, 6.38M, paid, `solid`](https://www.tiktok.com/@alondrarubyyy/video/7626924300246732063)), **Freshpet** ([@drmollysays, 2.5M, paid](https://www.tiktok.com/@drmollysays/video/7659508752365784334)), Farmer's Dog, Ollie, Open Farm, Victor (sensitive stomach), The Honest Kitchen, Wellness Core.

**Implication for our content:** scanning a **Purina** product is the single highest-engagement brand choice available — everyone already has an opinion. A neutral, science-cited score is the perfect "referee" wedge.
