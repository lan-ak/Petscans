# PetScans — Setting Up Your Accounts

**About 25 minutes. Do it in one sitting, on your own phone, on your home wifi — not a VPN.**

Four platforms: **Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat.** No Facebook — don't create a Facebook
profile or Page for this. All four are ordinary personal accounts. Nothing here is a business
account and nothing should look like one.

These accounts are PetScans' under clause 8.6 of your agreement: the handle, the login, and the
following. Your own existing accounts stay yours, and so do your portfolio rights and every bonus
you earn. We supply the emails, the passwords, the phone number, the photo, the bio and the link —
all in your Discord setup thread. Ask before you start if anything's missing.

### The one rule

**Never change the email, password, phone number, or 2FA on these accounts.** If something needs to
change, ask in Discord and we'll do it together. That's the whole of what keeps these recoverable.

---

### 1. Handle — before you create anything

Post 3 candidates in your thread. We pick one. **Check it's free on all four platforms before
claiming any of them**, then claim it everywhere the same day. Same handle on all four.

### 2. Your two email addresses

We send you two, and they do different jobs. Don't mix them up.

- **The YouTube one** is a Google account, and **you hold its password** — it's how you sign in to
  post. Use it for YouTube and nothing else.
- **The other one** is what Instagram, TikTok and Snapchat get registered to. **You'll never need to
  open it.** When one of them emails a verification code, ask in Discord and we'll paste it back
  within minutes.

Never use your personal email for any of the four, and never "Continue with Google/Facebook" pointed
at a personal account.

### 3. Create the four accounts

All of them on your own phone, on your home wifi. YouTube first, then Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat.

- **YouTube** — sign in to the Google account we gave you, then create the channel from your avatar →
  **Create a channel**. Leave it as an ordinary channel.
- **Instagram** — create it at **instagram.com in a browser**, not the app.
- **TikTok** — email and password, not phone-first.
- **Snapchat** — email and password, then create a **Public Profile**. This one isn't optional —
  without it you can't post publicly to Spotlight at all.

Then, for every one of them:

1. Verify the email. For Instagram, TikTok and Snapchat, ask in Discord for the code.
2. If it demands a phone number, use ours. If it rejects ours and you have to use yours, **say so in
   the thread** — we'll swap it off you later.
3. **Turn on 2FA with an authenticator app, not SMS.** On the QR screen, click "can't scan?" to get
   the **setup key** and paste it into the vault item *before you finish*. Then paste the **backup
   codes** it gives you into the same item's notes. Skipping either one is how accounts get lost for
   good — it's the most important 60 seconds in this document.
4. **Leave it as a personal account.** Don't switch Instagram to Professional, don't switch TikTok to
   Business, don't make the YouTube channel a Brand Account, and skip any prompt to connect a
   Facebook Page. Personal accounts keep the full audio library and get treated like a person by the
   feed, which is the whole point. If we ever need to run ads through one of your Instagram posts
   we'll ask you to flip that account to Creator for it — ten seconds, and it flips back.
5. TikTok only: turn on ad authorization under Settings → Creator tools. This works on a personal
   account and doesn't change your account type.

Post the handle in Discord as each goes live.

### 4. Set up all four profiles identically

The photo, display name, bio and tracked link we sent — same on all four, exactly as sent. Public,
comments on. No link-in-bio services, no affiliate links, nothing personal. Don't follow or link
these from your own accounts.

### 5. Record it — this is the part that matters

In the shared vault, one item per account plus one for each email, filled in **as you create it, not
later**: password, 2FA setup key, backup codes, the email and phone used, the date, and which device
you used.

**Nothing goes in Discord: no passwords, no setup keys, no recovery codes** — not in a channel, not
in a DM, not in a screenshot. If we ever ask you for one, we're wrong; say no. Six-digit
verification codes are fine, they expire in minutes.

Then post in your thread, per account: the handle, the profile link, and a screenshot of the live
profile. Takes thirty seconds and it's the record of when each account started.

### 6. Warm up before the first post

Two or three days of using each account like a person — scroll, follow 20–40 relevant accounts,
like things, finish videos. An account whose first ever action is a sponsored video with a link
gets treated like one.

### While you're running them

Post from one device; tell us before adding a second. Every video needs the disclosure from clause 6:
**#ad at the start** of the caption, above the "more" cut-off, plus spoken or on-screen in the first
3 seconds. Personal accounts don't have the paid-partnership toggle, so the caption and the on-screen
line are doing all the work — they aren't optional. Screenshot your view counts when we ask; that's
how bonuses get counted. Reply to comments like a person. Don't delete posts (clause 4.3 keeps them
up 12 months), don't post other brands, don't buy views or join pods (clause 7.4 voids the bonus and
risks the ban).

**If anything breaks — locked out, suspended, a strike, a login alert, phone lost — post it in
`#help` within 24 hours and don't act alone.** Don't retry a lockout, don't make a replacement
account on the same device, don't change a password mid-compromise. We have the recovery codes.
